Client Ledger Export Updates

- Client Ledger PDF export added.
- PDF and Excel exports omit the "Difference for date" and "All-time difference" columns.
- Excel export retains embedded Drive photos where the signed-in Google account can load them, and includes clickable Drive photo links.
- PDF export embeds available Drive photos and links photo cells to the Drive source.
- Added clearly titled all-time client balance table and date-labelled Received on / Sent on summaries in the PDF.
- Excel includes Client Ledger, Daily Summary, All-time Balance, and Sent and Received tabs; difference columns are removed from all sheets.
- Added Google Sheets export to a new spreadsheet, including transaction links to Drive photos, date summaries, balance table, and automatic Drive lookup if the create response omits its spreadsheet ID.
- Bumped dashboard asset cache versions to load the updated code.

Note: Google Sheets uses clickable Drive photo links because private Drive images cannot reliably render through a spreadsheet IMAGE() formula without changing the photo-sharing permissions. PDF and XLSX embed the available photos when enabled and accessible.
